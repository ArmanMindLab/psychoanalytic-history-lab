# Initial Literature Search for ISTDP and Attachment Theory

**Search Date:** June 14, 2026
**Focus:** Relationship between Intensive Short-Term Dynamic Psychotherapy (ISTDP) and Attachment Theory (Bowlby, Ainsworth, and modern developments)

## Key Papers (2024–2026)

1. **Effectiveness and mechanisms of Intensive Short-Term Dynamic Psychotherapy for treatment-resistant depression: a reanalysis of a randomized controlled trial** (2025)
   - PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42100787/
   - Key Finding: ISTDP significantly reduces emotional repression and negative affect. Attachment trauma is explicitly identified as a core etiological factor.
   - Relevance: Strong support for ISTDP as an attachment-informed intervention.

2. **Emotion regulation in short-term dynamic therapy: Attachment-moderated changes and bidirectional temporal associations with symptoms** (2025)
   - PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41343375/
   - Key Finding: Attachment style moderates emotion regulation changes in short-term dynamic therapy. Avoidant and anxious attachment show different trajectories.
   - Relevance: Direct empirical link between attachment patterns and outcomes in dynamic therapy (highly relevant to ISTDP).

3. **The relationship between defense mechanisms and attachment: a narrative review** (2024)
   - PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41645772/
   - Key Finding: Insecure attachment is strongly associated with immature defenses; secure attachment with mature defenses. Anxious attachment linked to projection/splitting, avoidant to suppression/denial.
   - Relevance: Foundational for understanding ISTDP’s focus on breaking defenses in the context of attachment trauma.

## Gap Analysis Summary
- Very few studies directly examine **ISTDP through an explicit attachment theory lens**.
- Most research treats attachment as a moderator or background variable rather than a central theoretical framework.
- Lack of longitudinal studies on how ISTDP changes adult attachment representations.
- No major review paper yet synthesizes ISTDP’s technical interventions with modern attachment research (e.g., mentalization, earned security, attachment-based emotion regulation).

**Conclusion:** There is a clear and valuable gap for a high-quality review or theoretical paper that bridges Davanloo’s ISTDP model with contemporary attachment theory and research.
